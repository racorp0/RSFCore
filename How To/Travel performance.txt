TravelSystem performance

TravelSystem.Update now queries GetEntitiesWith<TravelComponent>().ToList()
instead of scanning every world entity. The existing component index selects
active routes, skipping stationary terrain and NPCs without travel plans.
ToList copies only travelers because Stop removes TravelComponent during
arrival or invalidation. Iterating the live index directly would be unsafe.
No cache, new system, or replacement movement/pathfinding mechanism was added.

Start, Stop, and the existing update body are unchanged. Four-direction route
search, blocked/water checks, proximity, route queues, health/death/sleep checks,
movement intervals, and velocity updates continue through the existing code.
Program.cs, JSON settings, and simulation registration are unchanged.
Query order is not guaranteed; current traveler updates affect their own routes
and velocities, without collision reservations or shared movement resources.
Future collision or road-capacity features would need a separate ordering review.

Validation:
- Existing 95 map/gameplay checks passed, including routes to village locations,
  avoiding walls/water, and arrival at interaction points.
- Existing 27 survival AI and 20 profiler checks passed (142 total).
- A focused manual workflow stopped four invalid routes in one update and
  verified every route was removed and every velocity reset without skipping.
- Source comparison confirmed Start, Stop, and the full update body unchanged.
- Console build passed with 0 errors and 20 existing nullable warnings on initial
  compilation. Output used a temporary folder; no running app was stopped.
  No production tests were deleted or new test framework added.

Controlled Debug benchmark: 22,500 empty terrain entities, 480 updates, three
alternating original/updated pairs after warm-up. Travelers had valid routes
but were waiting for their next movement interval at a fixed clock.
Median milliseconds:
  3 travelers: original 638.513; updated 1.141.
  400 travelers: original 960.436; updated 151.771 (about 6.3 times faster).
The original source was copied before editing for comparison. This measures
route validation/selection only, not route finding or complete 400-NPC gameplay.

One-day village profile: 150x150 map, 3 residents, 1,440 game minutes.
  Previous run: wall 14,824.61 ms; TravelSystem 1,904.56 ms.
  Current run: wall 11,887.51 ms; TravelSystem 8.91 ms.
Full-day samples include random gameplay and runtime variation; their entire
wall-time difference cannot be attributed solely to this change. Route-finding
cost in Start is outside the per-system Update timing and was not optimized.
CookingSystem now leads the profile at 1,762.84 ms and remains future work.
