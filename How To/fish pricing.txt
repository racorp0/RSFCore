Fish pricing - phase 1

Set pricePer500Grams on each species in RSF.Console/Settings/fishing.json.
Starting rates: Carp 10, Catfish 12, Tilapia 8 gold per 500 grams.

Whole-fish sale price = (weightGrams * pricePer500Grams + 499) / 500.
This uses whole-number division, so adding 499 rounds a fraction of gold up.
At 10 gold per 500 g: 200 g costs 4, 500 g costs 10, 750 g costs 15,
and 1200 g costs 24 gold. Fish remain individual items when traded.

FishPricing.SalePrice is shared by both existing AI offer execution paths.
It uses long arithmetic before converting a valid result to int, preventing overflow.
Invalid fish or unknown species in a configured world return zero and are not offered.
Manual worlds without GameSettings use the NPC FishPrice as a per-500g fallback.
Existing manually constructed fish definitions default to 10 gold per 500 grams.

The loader rejects nonpositive rates and configured maximum fish prices exceeding
int.MaxValue. Existing posted offers are fixed until offered again, as before.
Manual OfferItem commands still accept an explicit custom price.
Cooking portions, catch chances, bait, and the Program.cs workflow are unchanged.
Bait types and river/lake/sea catch tables belong to later phases.
